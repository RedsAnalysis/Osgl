01 The Project

Barcelona-inspired.
Bengaluru-connected.

A Mediterranean address, designed around the way you actually live.

Set across 17 acres at Shettigere, Godrej MSR City brings the warmth of Mediterranean architecture to North Bengaluru's most connected corridor. Whitewashed forms, warm stone, shaded balconies and a landscape built for unhurried evenings — with the airport, the metro and the city's next business district all within easy reach.

Phase 2 introduces Barca, a collection of 2 and 3 BHK residences where natural light, cross-ventilation and open views shape everyday life. Developed by Godrej Properties, with possession planned from 2030.

Where exactly is Godrej MSR City?

At Shettigere, North Bengaluru, just off the Airport Road corridor (NH-44). The project address is 5MW6+366, Yerthiganahalli, Akkalenahalli Mallena - Halli, Shettigere, Bengaluru, Karnataka 562157.

What configurations and sizes are available?

Phase 2 offers 2 BHK and 3 BHK residences, including 2 BHK Luxe, 3 BHK + 2T Premium, 3 BHK Grand and 3 BHK Luxe, ranging from 1,200 to 2,275 sq.ft.

What is the starting price?

2 BHK residences start at ₹1.59 Cr* on an indicative pre-launch basis. Taxes, registration and other charges are extra. Request the current cost sheet on WhatsApp for a complete breakup.

When is possession?

Possession is planned from 2030 onwards for selected residences. Timelines are indicative and subject to approvals and construction progress.

Is the project RERA registered?

Yes. The RERA registration shown for this phase is PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/020626/006644 (BARCA AT GODREJ MSR CITY). Please verify the latest status at rera.karnataka.gov.in.

How far is the airport and the metro?

Kempegowda International Airport is approximately 9 km (about 15 minutes) away. The upcoming Doddajala Metro Station is approximately 6 km (about 12 minutes) away. NH-44 is about 2 km from the project.

Is OSGL Developers the developer of this project?

No. OSGL Developers is an authorised channel partner for Godrej MSR City. This is not the official website of Godrej Properties. We help with cost sheets, floor plans, site visits and the booking process.

Can I schedule a site visit?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 89711 81288 and we will arrange a site visit and share current availability.
