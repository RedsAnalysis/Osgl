Mediterranean-Inspired Living

Godrej MSR City at Shettigere, North Bangalore, brings a distinctive Mediterranean-inspired lifestyle to one of the city’s most connected residential locations. Set against the backdrop of Airport Road, the project introduces Barca at Godrej MSR City, where Barcelona-inspired design meets contemporary urban comfort.

Spread across a dedicated 17-acre development, the project offers thoughtfully planned 2 & 3 BHK apartments designed around natural light, open spaces and everyday functionality. Sunlit landscapes, breezy balconies and vibrant lifestyle amenities create an atmosphere inspired by the relaxed charm of Mediterranean living.

Project Highlights

  • Mediterranean-inspired residences blending Barcelona charm with contemporary urban living.
  • Located in Shettigere, along the well-connected Airport Road corridor.
  • Dedicated 17-acre development featuring thoughtfully planned 2 and 3 BHK apartments.
  • Kempegowda International Airport is approximately 9 km from the project.
  • Upcoming Doddajala Metro Station is approximately 6 km away.
  • Possession is planned from 2030 onwards for selected residences.
